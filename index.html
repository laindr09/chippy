Grown slow, poured with care

Chippy’s Brew started with a simple idea: a coffee shop should feel like a favorite chair. Warm wood, soft light, and a cup made the way you like it.

We pick our beans carefully, roast in small batches, and bake fresh every morning, so every visit tastes like the first one.
